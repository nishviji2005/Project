Hello, we're Nish Bakes

From a home oven to your celebration table.

Nish Bakes started in a small kitchen with one mixing bowl and a lot of stubbornness about doing things properly. What began as birthday cakes for friends has grown into a home bakery trusted for weddings, showers and everyday sweetness — but the recipe hasn't changed: real ingredients, no shortcuts, and a cake that tastes as good as it looks.

Every order is baked in small batches, so nothing waits around — your cake is mixed, baked and iced within a day of your pickup or delivery slot.

Our Journey

How it all rose

2021

First cake, first customer

A neighbour's birthday order turned into a waiting list of ten within a month.

2025

Moved to a dedicated kitchen

Upgraded from a home oven to a licensed baking space to keep up with orders.

2025

300th cake delivered

Celebrated our own milestone the only way we know how — with cake, obviously.

Today

Baking for the whole city

Now delivering custom cakes, cupcakes and dessert jars across Kanyakumari district, every week.
